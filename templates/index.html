About Optocare

Optocare is an online optical platform connecting you to trusted eye care professionals and high-quality optical products—no matter where you are. We partner with leading eye care providers to ensure you receive professional, reliable, and accessible eye care services.

We don’t just focus on vision correction—we focus on style, comfort, and confidence because we believe you should always look and feel your best.

Our clients include:

  • Individuals requiring vision correction support
  • Clients seeking fashion eyewear and sunglasses
  • Users interested in contact lenses (cosmetic or corrective)
  • Eye care practitioners needing optical tools and equipment
  • Clinics training staff on modern optical practices
  • Optical stores connecting with global eyewear brands
  • Practices upgrading diagnostic machines and systems

Operating from the UAE gives us a strategic advantage and enables strong international reach, allowing us to provide consultation and access to global optical brands.
